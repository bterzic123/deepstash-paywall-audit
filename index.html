What this is

We installed Deepstash, went through onboarding like a new user, and set your paywall next to the report.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what moves conversion across the market rather than inside one app. We picked a focus, goals and topics, chose our first books, opened the paywall, tried both plans, closed it, and read your App Store listing. Then we compared what we saw with State of Paywalls 2026.

What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype. The prototype uses your design, not ours. Benchmarks are for Education apps across all regions: Education is your App Store category, and no single region makes up most of your revenue.

We did not redesign your app

The prototype keeps your dark screen, your plan cards with the blue trial badge and the green “Save 64%”, your grey button, your red 3D feature icons and your own lines. We changed what the paywall shows and what happens when someone closes it, using only prices, claims and a discount you already publish.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If you have already run one of these, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest.

The five ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes come first, because the report finds they win A/B tests more often than visual ones. Each row opens the screen in the prototype.

01

Give the close a second chance

Tap X today and the next screen asks how you heard about Deepstash, then the app opens. Show one offer first: the 45% off Yearly you ran on Deepstash and Paladin in July, which makes €99.99 into €54.99.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 4% of apps do this; it converts at 0.88× the main paywall.

02

Show both plans on the first screen

Monthly at €22.99 only appears if someone taps “View all plans”. Put both of your plan cards on the paywall, Yearly selected with its free week. Then €8.33 a month reads as the deal it is: 64% less.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · Plan-count changes win 57% of A/B tests on LTV; visual or copy changes win 35%.

03

Put your 4.8 and 10 million readers on the paywall

Your loading screen shows a 4.8 rating from 100K+ ratings, 10,000,000+ people in the community, and Forbes, Mashable, Macworld and Entrepreneur. A few seconds later the paywall shows none of it, and in our session its top 40% stayed empty. That space is where the proof goes.

04

Name what they picked in the headline

Onboarding asks for a focus, goals and at least three topics. Then the paywall says the same sentence to everyone. A headline like “Keep growing in Philosophy & Mindfulness” uses the answers they gave a minute earlier.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 2% of top paywalls use what the app knows about the user.

05

Hold the X for three seconds

The X is there the moment the paywall opens. Showing it after three seconds, with a small ring where it will appear, gives the plans and the proof time to be read before the way out.
